[S3] ADMISSION APPLICATION PROCEDURE - 2026-27
1. Register on the university application portal using basic personal details and verify email and mobile number.
2. Complete the five form sections: Personal Details; Parent/Guardian Details; Correspondence Details; Academic Details; Document Upload.
3. Complete all five sections and submit the form with an application fee of INR 1800 to proceed with evaluation and admission processing.
The application process is not a guarantee of admission. This sheet does not specify an application deadline, entrance-test cutoffs, seat availability, or a final admission decision.
Source: https://mituniversity.ac.in/Admissions/mitadt_admission_process

[S4] B.TECH ELIGIBILITY - SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING AND SCIENCES ONLY
Passed 10+2 from State Board, CBSE, ICSE or IB with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and/or Technical Vocational Subjects.
Open category: minimum 50 percent in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, with English as a subject.
Reserved category: minimum 45 percent in Physics, Chemistry/Vocational and Mathematics, with English as a subject.
These published criteria apply to the School of Engineering and Sciences page. This sheet does not verify separate School of Computing CSE eligibility or entrance examination requirements; contact admissions for those details.
